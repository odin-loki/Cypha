Analysis `hanna_corr` (5. Human ratings (HANNA, OpenMEVA)). meta.py `corr`.
Inputs: SP/data/hanna/texts.jsonl, SP/run/human/hanna.v2.jsonl, SP/run/human/hanna.v2p.jsonl, SP/run/ts/hanna.ts.jsonl, SP/data/hanna/shipped_metrics.jsonl, SP/run/baselines/hanna/stats.jsonl, SP/run/baselines/hanna/ppl_gpt2.jsonl.
Shipped metrics: Ξ marks a metric that compares with the human reference story (so it is degenerate on the human stories themselves), ¤ a reference-free one. Directions: higher is better except BaryScore, DepthScore, InfoLM Fisher-Rao (distances) and Repetition-n; Coverage, Density and Compression have no natural direction and are taken as higher is better.

1056 texts, ratings under `meta.human`, criteria: RE, CH, EM, SU, EG, CX. Item level: Pearson, Spearman and Kendall tau-b between the oriented metric and the mean rating; percentile intervals from 1000 resamples of the items (seed 20261028). Δ Kendall is the item-level Kendall minus that of the reference metric (SCRIBE v2) on the same items; its p-value is a two-sided paired bootstrap test (centred: p = (1 + #|d* − d| >= |d|) / (B + 1)), Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted across metrics within each criterion. System level: Kendall and Spearman between per-system mean ratings and per-system mean metric scores, intervals from resampling items within systems. Human ceiling: one rating of each item, drawn at random, against the mean of its other ratings (leave one out), averaged over 20 draws; it compares one rater with k − 1, so it is a conservative reference for a metric compared with the mean of all k.
