## What this era is

Three parallel design dialogues, each bolting a new faculty onto an **already-existing Cell
AI learning engine that is not in the archive**. The opening line of the longest log states
the relationship exactly:

> So I want to make a thinking pattern that bolts onto this AIs. This AI is based off a
> learning pattern.
> — `Cell AI v2 Thinking Convo Log.txt:1`

and the Brain Model thread opens the same way:

> So I want to make an AI of sorts from patterns of Neural Resonances to go with this
> Cell AI... I want models of brain cells to go together with that to make an AI.
> — `Brain Model Convo Log.txt:1`

So the archive begins *after* the beginning. Cypha's actual root — a Cell AI that already
learned — is lost. What survives is the record of giving it a thinking layer.

## Thread 1 — Brain Model

"Dynamic Recursive Brain Architecture". A five-level state stack
`Ψ(t) = {C(t), A(t), M(t), O(t), G(t)}` — Cells → Assemblies → Modules → Oscillations →
Global — with three declared recursion types: horizontal (within-level), vertical
(between-level), and temporal/predictive, `X(t) = f(X(t−Δt), X̂(t+Δt|t))`.

The cell equation is a diffusion-augmented recurrent net with global feedback:

```
dcᵢ/dt = Aᵢcᵢ + BᵢIᵢ + ∑ⱼ[W_ij(t)σ(c_j)] + Dᵢ∇²cᵢ + Rᵢ(G,cᵢ) + ηᵢ(t)
```
— `Brain Model Math Model.md:51`

with event-gated jumps `cᵢ(t⁺) = cᵢ(t) + Δcᵢ(E,G)·[hᵢ(E,G) > θᵢ(t)]` and a multi-timescale
learning rule mixing exponential-kernel Hebbian terms with a prediction-error term.

This thread is the most literally biological, and almost none of it survives. It hard-codes
species-specific modules (insect navigation, mammalian CA3/EC/DG place cells, cephalopod
central and arm controllers), a neuromodulator 4-vector
`M_mod = [DA, NE, 5HT, ACh]ᵀ` (`:230`), and an ERK/CREB biochemical cascade as the unit of
memory. None of it reappears in v2, v3, or anything later.

## Thread 2 — Cell AI v2 "Thinking"

The thread that matters most, because its two central decisions propagate all the way to
the native product.

**Events become a first-class typed primitive.** `e = (type, location, time, priority, data)`
over `T × X × R⁺ × P × D` with `P = [0,1]`, generated by
`P(e|S,t) = G(S,t)·H(θ,t)·W(C,t)` and inter-arrival `t_next ~ Exp(λ(priority))`.

**The inherited field equation is then gated by those events**, turning a dense PDE into a
sparse one:

```
dS(x,t)/dt = ∑ₑ E(e,x,t) × [F(S,e) + D(e)∇²S + W(S,e) + A(S,e) + O(S,e)]

    with sparse computation:  ∂S/∂t ≠ 0  only where  E(e,x,t) > 0
```
— `Cell AI v2 Thinking Math Model.md:51-62`

That last line is the era's single most consequential sentence: computation happens only
where an event is live.

The structural principle is **one equation, six instantiations**. Training, inference,
shard-local processing, and all three "thinking speeds" run the *same* field equation,
differing only in the event filter. The diagram encodes this literally as dotted reuse
edges:

```
FE1 -.-> |Same Equation| TPF1 & IPF1 & SPF1 & FPF1 & APF1 & DPF1
```
— `Cell AI v2 Thinking Architecture.mermaid:83`

Thinking speeds are mapped onto EEG bands *and onto scheduler priority classes at the same
time*: gamma 35–80 Hz = urgent queue, beta 12–35 Hz = normal, alpha/theta 4–12 Hz =
background. "Multi-speed thinking" and "priority scheduling" are made one mechanism.

**Astrocytes replace graphs.** Asked what substrate should carry events, the dialogue lands
on astrocyte calcium waves:

> YES! Astrocytes are perfect for this! [...] They use calcium waves for signaling (which
> is like a natural event system)
> — `Cell AI v2 Thinking Convo Log.txt:1047` and `:1051`

giving a reaction–diffusion event medium
`∂Ca/∂t = D_Ca∇²Ca + f(Ca,E) − γCa + ∑ₑ I(e,x,t)` with thresholded release and
threshold-crossing event birth `E_new(x,t) = Θ(C(x,t) − C_threshold)` — a closed
generate → propagate → regenerate loop. This was a deliberate removal, not an accident:

> I wanted to ditch the graphs for more natural maths, that was more dynamic and still did
> the same thing.
> — `Cell AI v2 Thinking Convo Log.txt:2298`

### The graphs came back, as an opt-in variant

Sixteen months later the current tree carries a graph cell again:

```cpp
{"H23", "PGM cell", 3, true, "hybrid",
 "Plastic Graph Machine: hierarchical log-N address + sparse slot graph (no dense attn)"},
```
— `native/src/cyphalm/cypha_cell_hypothesis.cpp:37-38`

It is implemented rather than declared — `native/src/cyphalm/pgm_cell.cpp`, plus four
unified-context variants built on it (U04 PGM→LSTM, U05 PGM→GRIA, U06 PGM→logits, U09
PGM-large) — and it is reachable when switched on. But it is not in the product. The
production recipe does not merely leave it at its default; it writes the flag off explicitly:

```cpp
cfg.use_pgm_cell = false;
cfg.use_unified_context = false;
```
— `native/src/cyphalm/cyphalm_config.cpp:97-98`

and it did not win its bake-off. The 2026-07-18 head-to-head reports H23 as indistinguishable
from the hybrid baseline — the report's own conclusion is that "PGM is **not yet a BPC
competitor** as wired: no BPTT into the graph, no replacement of the LSTM head, field path
nearly muted."

Two caveats on reading this as a reversal. The BPC figures in those reports could not be
reproduced here — no build tree exists and the result artifacts are gitignored and absent — so
they are report-asserted, not independently confirmed. And the roles differ: 2025 abandoned
graphs as the *reasoning substrate*, replacing them with astrocyte calcium waves, whereas H23's
sparse slot graph sits inside a memory cell. The idea returned; the architectural position did
not.

The dialogue also fixes what "cell" means in this project, and it is not biology:

> A good one is cells. What happens in cells? They "think" in ways and so does the brain...
> What we are looking for is a model that is bastardised to be computational. It wont be an
> exact match to that system.
> — `Cell AI v2 Thinking Convo Log.txt:1015`

Finally, the model is treated as a **distributed database**: separate training and
inference pathways, sharding, replication by delta update `ΔR = C(R′ − R)`, event sourcing,
and eventual consistency with a *bounded* inconsistency budget `max_{i,j}||Sᵢ − Sⱼ|| ≤ δ(t)`.

## Thread 3 — Cell AI v3 / OICFHS

"Optimized Integrated Cell-Fungal Harmonic System" — the merge of Cell AI v2 with the
unarchived Fungal NA, and the most implementation-shaped document of the three (it carries
pseudocode, a record schema and complexity annotations).

Master equation:

```
∂Ψ/∂t = H(Ψ) + R_enhanced(Ψ,ρ) + N(Ψ,τ) + C(Ψ,κ)
```

The novel operator is a three-term resonance with a **multiplicative cross term**:

```
R_enhanced = α·R_direct + β·R_harmonic + γ·R_coupled
    R_direct   = exp(−δ‖Ψ − template(ω)‖²)              fast Gaussian template match
    R_harmonic = A₀/√[(ω₀²−ω²)² + (ω²/Q²)]              the inherited Lorentzian
    R_coupled  = R_direct × R_harmonic                   cross-coupling
```
— `Cell AI v3 Math Model.md:22-29`

The cross term exists so that the cheap template path *primes* rather than *bypasses* the
expensive harmonic path. It was written in response to the only empirical measurement
anywhere in this era:

> When I profiled the origional cell v2 it wasnt very good at simple patterns?
> — `Cell AI v3 Convo Log.txt:3073`

This produces the **three-tier early-exit recognition cascade** — Fast Signature Matcher →
Enhanced Resonance → Full Resonance, each tier passing candidates and context down — which
is the shape every later Cypha classifier keeps.

Other durable ideas from this thread:

- **HLFC** (Harmonic Lattice-Folded Compression): four multiplicatively stacked layers with
  stated ratios and complexities — fundamental extraction 50:1 `O(n log n)`, symmetry
  encoding 20:1 `O(s)`, crystal-lattice defect mapping 50:1 `O(d)`, harmonic folding 100:1
  `O(log f)`. The name survives into the v3/v4 module docstrings.
- **Metastable states** `Ψ_meta = {(Ψᵢ, pᵢ)}` with Bayesian reweighting — carrying multiple
  live hypotheses instead of committing to one label (`:196-208`).
- **Dynamic resonator birth and death** by novelty threshold: capacity grows and prunes
  rather than being fixed.
- **Learned non-integer harmonic ratios**: `r′ₘ = rₘ + η∇_{rₘ}(∑_p |R(rₘω₀,p)|²)`.
- **Criticality as the resonator's damping term**,
  `R_crit = A₀/√[(ω₀²−ω²)² + (κω)²]`, making self-organised criticality and resonance
  sharpening one knob.
- **Single-core intent.** §7.2 is titled "Optimized Single-Core Implementation" —
  prioritised processing, lazy evaluation, progressive precision, chunked processing,
  adaptive scheduling, with SIMD/GPU explicitly optional. This is the first appearance of
  the cache-conscious, single-core mindset that eventually makes a C++ port the obvious
  move.

## Claims that do not hold up

The archive should be read with these flagged. They are recorded here so later readers do
not inherit them uncritically.

- **"~500,000:1 practical compression"** (`Cell AI v3 Math Model.md:483`) and
  **"50,000:1"** (`Cell AI v2 Thinking Intro.md:11`). No measurement, dataset, corpus or
  reconstruction-fidelity criterion appears anywhere in the corpus. The 500,000:1 figure is
  the product of four independently asserted per-layer ratios (50 × 20 × 50 × 100 =
  5,000,000) divided by ten under the unexplained word "practical".
- **"Total theoretical enhancement: ~10¹⁶×"** (`Cell AI v2 Thinking Intro.md:79`). The
  intro lists three factors — 10⁴, 10³, 10³ — which multiply to **10¹⁰**, not 10¹⁶. The
  document is internally inconsistent, and neither "enhancement" nor its baseline is ever
  defined.
- **"~20-50× system speedup on single core"** and **"100-200× faster on a single core"**,
  both asserted in `Cell AI v3 Convo Log.txt` without reconciliation.
- **Per-complexity speedups** ("simple ~1000-2000× faster", `Cell AI v3 Math Model.md:479-482`)
  were generated inside the dialogue before any implementation existed.
- **The free-energy definition** `F = E[ln p(Ψ|θ)] − H[q(θ)]` (`Cell AI v3 Math Model.md:450`)
  is not variational free energy, which is `F = E_q[ln q(θ) − ln p(Ψ,θ)]`. The sign of the
  first term is inverted and the expectation's measure is unspecified.
- **The predictive-coding update** `∂Ψ/∂t = Ψ − G(Ψ) − α·ε` with `ε = Ψ − G(Ψ)`
  (`:460-465`) reduces to `∂Ψ/∂t = (1−α)ε`, which does not contract unless `α > 1`.
- **`H(Ψ) = −(ℏ²/2m)∇²Ψ + V(x)Ψ`** (`:15`) imports ℏ and m into a computational model
  where neither is ever given a value, units or interpretation.
- **Section numbering in `Cell AI v3 Math Model.md` is corrupt** — "## 10. Computational
  Efficiency" is followed by "### 9.1" and "### 9.2", and section 9 appears twice. Cite
  line numbers, not section numbers, from that file.

## What reached the native product

Very little of the specific mathematics, but three structural commitments survive
thirteen months and a complete architectural restart:

1. **Sparse, event-gated computation** — work happens only where something has changed.
2. **The early-exit cascade** — a cheap path that primes rather than bypasses an expensive
   one; visible today in the native inference path's staged scoring.
3. **Single-core, cache-conscious design as a first-class goal**, not an afterthought.

The biology — astrocytes, neuromodulators, ERK/CREB, species modules — is entirely gone.
